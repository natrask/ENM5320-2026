Final Project — ENM 5320

Overview

The final project will require a 2–3 page written report and a short (<5 min) presentation on the last day of class. Reports may be turned in any time prior to the closing of grades if you need some extra time.

You can work on anything you want that is relevant to:

  • PDEs
  • Finite differences / finite elements
  • Dynamical systems
  • Physics-based modeling
  • Machine learning

You are welcome to work in groups of 1–4. The project is intentionally open-ended. Take this as an opportunity to pursue your interests and get 1-on-1 feedback from Prof. Trask on your research.

Grading Criteria

Projects will be graded on the following:

  1. Clarity — Are sufficient details provided for us to understand what you did?
  2. Scientific rigor — Did you articulate and address a clear scientific hypothesis?
  3. Supporting evidence — Did you submit code/math that validates your writeup?

Attribution Policy

Acknowledge all contributions, including AI. Projects lacking proper attribution will receive a zero.
